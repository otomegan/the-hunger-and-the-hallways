# Visual Novel Lore Keeper: The Hunger and the Hallways

This skill acts as the canonical lore keeper for the dark gothic universe of *The Hunger and the Hallways*. Every piece of world-building, rule of magic, bestiary entry, and historical lore must strictly harmonize with the principles established here.

---

## 1. Fundamental Cosmology & The Hunger

### The Nature of "The Hunger" (A Fome)
- The Hunger is **not** glamorous romantic vampirism. It is an insidious, parasitic existential atrophy.
- **The Infection:** It creeps into the bone marrow. It sharpens the senses to an agonizing degree—victims hear carotid arteries thumping three rooms away and smell iron in a drop of spilled saliva.
- **The Paradox:** Consuming blood/flesh provides euphoric relief and superhuman vitality (`ataque_jogador`, `energia_mistica`), but directly erodes psychological humanity (`humanidade`). Starving preserves morality at the cost of delirium, agonizing physical pain, and predatory frenzy (`overlay_frenesi_vermelho`).

### The Currency of Debts
- In this academy, power is never donated; it is loaned.
- Blood, secret documents, alibis, and survival items form a financial and metaphysical credit network (`livro_de_dividas` / `livro_de_creditos`).
- Defaulting on a debt to an aristocrat or an inquisitor invites systemic execution or ritual sacrifice.

## 2. Geography of the Institute

### Underground / O Subsolo (Domain of Steam and Blood)
- **As Caldeiras:** A maze of superheated pipes, iron grating, and leaking steam valves (`sfx_caldeiras_vapor_chiado.ogg`). Valéria’s stronghold. Noise here masks violence; industrial heat dehydrates the living.
- **Quarentena & Túneis de Descarte:** Dark forgotten shafts where failed students and desiccated husks are dumped.

### Ground Level / O Térreo (Institutional Order)
- **Grande Hall & Corredores de Mármore:** Vast, cold, echoic cathedral corridors (`sfx_passos_marmore_colegio.ogg`). Polished stone reflecting moonlight and gas lamps. Monitored closely by prefects and patrol dogs.
- **Refeitório:** A site of psychological tension; spoiled rations for scholarship students, private dining for aristocrats.
- **A Capela dos Exorcistas:** Desecrated stained glass, smell of burnt myrrh and dry wax. Emilia’s sanctuary of sorrow.

### Second Floor / O Segundo Andar (Forbidden Archives)
- **Biblioteca Oculta:** Bound grimoires, forbidden botanical catalogues, dust, whispers.
- **O Banheiro Abandonado:** Closed after a student suicide decades ago. The cracked full-length mirror is an active threshold (`sfx_sussurros_espelho_banheiro.ogg`). Whispers from the glass echo forgotten names and impending doom.

### Third Floor & Towers / Torres e Santuários (The Elite)
- **Sala do Grêmio Estudantil:** Immaculate mahogany, brass clockwork, official execution stamps. Elara’s domain.
- **Aposentos de Lility / Sala de Música:** Heavy velvet drapes, antique harpsichord, intoxicating scent of orchids and vintage port wine. Total absence of direct daylight.
- **Torre do Sino:** Highest vantage point, where curfew bells signal the hunt.

## 3. Bestiary & Entity Compendium

### Inquisitorial Forces & School Authorities
- **Diretor Ambrósio:** The bureaucratic tyrant who administrates the slaughter through school codes and academic probation.
- **Executora Nora:** High-ranking enforcer. Implacable, armored, wielding execution implements. Speaks with chilling professional detachment.
- **Cães de Sangue (Hellhounds):** Skinless biological hounds bred to hunt down starving students who break curfew (`sfx_hellhound_rosnar_gutural.ogg`). They do not bark like domestic dogs—they produce deep vibrational snarls and wet bone-cracking snaps.

### Urban Legends & Aberrations
- **Dama do Espelho / Loira do Banheiro:** Entity bound to reflective surfaces and flooded porcelain. Beckons through fractured memories.
- **Mulher Gigante (Hachishaku):** Towering figure stalking the foggy courtyards and colonnades; emits low rhythmic guttural warnings (*"Po... po... po..."*).
- **Marionete de Sangue:** Autonomous corpse animated by aristocratic blood threads.
- **A Fada Verde:** Hallucinatory manifestation of alchemical absinthe and distilled neurotoxins.

## 4. Lore Keeper Verification Rule

When adding new lore, relics, or historical events:
1. **Never break the rules of mortality:** Even supernatural beings have vulnerabilities (fire, consecrated silver, territorial anchors, blood starvation).
2. **Preserve the claustrophobic dread:** The school is an inescapable island isolated from the outside world. Any suggestion of easy escape ruins the stakes.
3. **Internal logic of horror:** Supernatural elements must always have visceral physical consequences—smell of sulfur, copper taste, bone chills, auditory pressure.
