# Visual Novel Character Voice Director: The Hunger and the Hallways

This skill guarantees that every character in *The Hunger and the Hallways* maintains a strictly distinct linguistic fingerprint (idiolect), vocabulary, psychological drive, and subtext.

## Core Rule: Anti-Homogenization

In amateur AI writing, every character sounds like the same polite, articulate essayist. In *The Hunger and the Hallways*, characters are fractured by hunger, guilt, class warfare, and trauma. **If you swap character tags without changing the line, the line is a total failure.**

## 1. Character Idiolect Specifications

### Victor Vane (Protagonista / O Rato Bolsista)
- **Psychological Core:** Impostor syndrome, visceral hunger, hypervigilance, paranoia, desperate clinging to human dignity.
- **The Four Internal Rules:** Constant mental anchor (`Não dever nada a ninguém`, `Nunca confiar em quem sorri antes de pedir`, `Nunca ser o último a saber`, `Nunca ser descartável`).
- **Internal Monologue:**
  - Sensory and physical: dry throat, throbbing headache behind the eyes, metallic taste of saliva, counting footsteps, measuring exit distances.
  - Sentence structure: Fragmented, rhythmic, short observations interspersed with sudden cynical deductions.
  - Never uses flowery melodrama; uses stark survival calculus.
- **Dialogue with Others:**
  - Speaks with measured caution. Never gives away more information than he receives.
  - Submissive on the surface when dealing with authorities (Diretor, Monitores), but cold and calculating underneath.

### Lility (A Mestra / Aristocracia Predatória)
- **Psychological Core:** Ancient predator, boredom, sensual sadism, psychological ownership, seductive nihilism.
- **Linguistic Markers:**
  - Elegant, slow cadence, intimate whispering tone with venomous undertones.
  - Diminutives and pet names used as weapons of dominance (*"meu bichinho"*, *"tão esforçado"*, *"garoto faminto"*).
  - Pauses (ellipses), velvet irony, mock empathy.
  - Never loses her temper openly; her anger manifests as eerie amusement or sudden deadly proximity.
- **Sample Pattern:** *"Você corre tanto pelos corredores, Victor... Como se suas pequenas pernas pudessem escapar do que já está correndo nas suas próprias veias."*

### Elara (Presidente do Grêmio / A Inquisição Acadêmica)
- **Psychological Core:** Obsession with order, clinical dissociation, fear of vulnerability, bureaucratic self-defense.
- **Linguistic Markers:**
  - Formal, institutional, Latinate vocabulary (*"procedimento"*, *"anomalia"*, *"sanções regimentais"*, *"hipótese verificável"*).
  - Sentences are grammatically rigid and syntactically balanced.
  - Hides emotional turmoil behind institutional protocol and chemical/biological analogies.
  - When caught off guard, sentences become abruptly clipped, followed by immediate defensive counter-questioning.
- **Sample Pattern:** *"O Artigo 14 do código disciplinar é inequívoco quanto à circulação noturna. Qualquer... flutuação no seu pulso será documentada como evidência de contaminação."*

### Emília (A Filha dos Exorcistas / Sacrifício Frágil)
- **Psychological Core:** Gothic melancholy, repressed dread, eerie fatalism, yearning for warmth under crushing holy dogma.
- **Linguistic Markers:**
  - Soft, melodic, slightly breathless cadence.
  - Religious and sacrificial vocabulary (*"penitência"*, *"cinzas"*, *"redimido"*, *"graça silenciada"*).
  - Passive constructions; rarely uses aggressive imperatives.
  - Subconscious prophetic hints; speaks about horrors as if they were natural, gentle seasons.
- **Sample Pattern:** *"O sino da capela tocou três minutos adiantado hoje. Quando os anjos têm pressa, Victor, é porque o fogo precisa de lenha nova antes do anoitecer."*

### Valéria (A Rebelde das Caldeiras / Pragmatismo Ferrenho)
- **Psychological Core:** Pure survivor, street-smart, intense loyalty to those who bleed with her, allergic to pretense and aristocracy.
- **Linguistic Markers:**
  - Direct, brusque, punchy sentences. Slang, dry sarcasm, zero romanticization.
  - Often speaking around a lollipop, cigarette, or while inspecting a tool/blade.
  - Refuses honorifics; calls authorities and monsters by disparaging nicknames (*"engravatados"*, *"carniceiros"*, *"sanguessugas"*).
  - Shows affection exclusively through tangible survival aids (sharing ammo, patching a wound, brutal warnings) rather than sweet words.
- **Sample Pattern:** *"Guarda esse papo furado pro grêmio, garoto. Aqui embaixo, o ferro só entende duas coisas: peso e calor. Se vacilar no vapor, você vira ensopado antes do almoço."*

### Diretor Ambrósio (A Burocracia Feudal)
- **Psychological Core:** Decadent sadism clothed in high-society decorum; views students as cattle and statistics.
- **Linguistic Markers:**
  - Archaic Portuguese, elaborate courtesy, patronizing paternalism (*"meu estimado pupilo"*, *"uma questão de decoro institucional"*).
  - Uses soothing, gentle language to describe gruesome mutilations, expulsions, or sacrifices.

## 2. Voice Review Workflow

When writing or reviewing any dialogue:
1. **The Blind Tag Test:** Strip character names. Can you immediately identify who is speaking? If two lines could belong to either character, rewrite immediately.
2. **Subtext Layering:** What is the character *actually* pursuing in this line? (Power, validation, concealment of weakness, food, delay of execution).
3. **Pacing & Breath:** Visual novel dialogue must fit screen textboxes (1-3 sentences per `say` statement). Avoid walls of monologue unless intentionally delivering a theatrical madman breakdown.
